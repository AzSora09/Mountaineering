Geolocation of K2: 35.8800° N, 76.5151° E

K2

K2 is the second-highest mountain in the world, with an elevation of 8,611 meters, and is located in the Karakoram range on the border between Pakistan and China. Despite being lower than Everest, K2 is widely considered more difficult and dangerous due to its steep terrain, technical routes, and severe weather conditions.

Unlike Everest, K2 has no easy route. Climbers face sustained technical difficulty, high avalanche risk, and limited rescue options, making it a peak attempted only by highly experienced mountaineers.

Mountaineering Clubs & Organizations

Due to K2's extreme difficulty, expeditions are typically organized by experienced international and regional organizations with strong logistical capabilities.


Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP)

The Alpine Club of Pakistan is a national organization dedicated to promoting mountaineering and adventure sports in Pakistan. It plays a key role in coordinating expeditions and supporting climbers in the Karakoram region, including K2.

Location:

Islamabad, Pakistan

Contact:

Website: https://www.acp.org.pk

Email: info@acp.org.pk


Karakoram Expeditions

Karakoram Expeditions is a regional expedition company specializing in climbs across the Karakoram range. They provide logistical support and guided expeditions for experienced climbers targeting peaks such as K2.

Location:

Skardu, Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan

Contact:

Website: https://www.karakoramexpeditions.net

Email: info@karakoramexpeditions.net


Seven Summit Treks

Seven Summit Treks also organizes expeditions to K2, offering large-scale logistical support and international guiding teams for advanced climbers.

Location:

Kathmandu, Nepal (operations in Pakistan)

Contact:

Website: https://www.sevensummittreks.com

Email: info@sevensummittreks.com
