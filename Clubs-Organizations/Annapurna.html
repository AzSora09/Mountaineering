Geolocation of Annapurna: 28.6136° N, 83.8736° E

Annapurna

Annapurna is a major Himalayan mountain located in north-central Nepal, with its highest peak, Annapurna I, reaching an elevation of 8,091 meters. It is one of the world's fourteen eight-thousanders and is widely regarded as one of the most dangerous mountains to climb due to its steep terrain and high avalanche risk.

Despite being lower than Everest and K2, Annapurna has a history of difficult ascents caused by unstable snow conditions, frequent avalanches, and rapidly changing weather. These factors make it a peak attempted primarily by highly skilled mountaineers.
