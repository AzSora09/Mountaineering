Nanga Parbat

Geolocation of Nanga Parbat: 35.2375° N, 74.5891° E

Nanga Parbat

Nanga Parbat is the ninth-highest mountain in the world, rising to an elevation of 8,126 meters, and is located in the western Himalayas of Pakistan. It is renowned for its dramatic relief and extreme difficulty, earning the nickname “The Killer Mountain” due to its challenging climbing history.

The mountain is especially known for its Rupal Face, one of the tallest mountain faces in the world. Climbers attempting Nanga Parbat face steep terrain, unstable weather, and limited rescue options, making it suitable only for highly experienced mountaineers.

Mountaineering Clubs & Organizations

Expeditions to Nanga Parbat are generally organized by regional and international groups experienced in high-altitude and technical climbing.


Alpine Club of Pakistan (ACP)

The Alpine Club of Pakistan is a national organization dedicated to promoting mountaineering and adventure sports in Pakistan. It plays a key role in coordinating expeditions and supporting climbers in the Karakoram region, including K2.

Islamabad, Pakistan

Karakoram Expeditions

Karakoram Expeditions is a regional expedition company specializing in climbs across the Karakoram range. They provide logistical support and guided expeditions for experienced climbers targeting peaks such as K2.
