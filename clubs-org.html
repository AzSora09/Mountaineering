CLUBS & ORGANIZATIONS

Discover the peaks that inspire, and see which clubs are conquering them next.

Major mountains are shown here along with the mountaineering communities and organizations connected to them.

Mount Everest

Mount Everest is the highest mountain on Earth and stands as one of the most iconic destinations in mountaineering, attracting climbers from around the world.

K2

K2 is the second-highest mountain in the world and is widely regarded as one of the most challenging peaks due to its steep routes and extreme conditions.

Nanga Parbat

Nanga Parbat is a massive Himalayan peak known for its prominent faces and reputation as one of the most difficult mountains to climb.

Annapurna

Annapurna is a major Himalayan mountain recognized for its technical terrain and history of demanding ascents.
